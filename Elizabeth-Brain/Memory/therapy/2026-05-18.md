## Key Insights

- 20+ years of inadequacy messaging from environment — current beliefs stem from others, not self-chosen
- Daily affirmation to practice: *"I no longer operate from guilt and shame. I operate from peace and love for myself"*
- Home environment creates "noise" that drowns out positive self-talk — getting outside breaks the loop
- Reframe question-asking: not "dumb questions" but "questions worth asking"
- Sister's external confidence may mask internal battles; her dating patterns reflect her own self-worth issues
- Small promise-keeping builds self-trust — focus on achievable daily commitments
- Avoid "have to" language; use "want to" instead — recognize agency in every decision

## Family Dynamics

- Sister relationship: focus on controlling own actions, not her responses; option to end unhealthy conversations
