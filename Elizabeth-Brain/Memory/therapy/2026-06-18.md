## Key Insights

- Lack of independence in decision-making traced to childhood: frequent moving, sister calling her dumb, controlling father
- Attachment style is likely anxious — shows up as clinginess, seeking validation before deciding
- No single soulmate exists; compatible partners are built over time through willingness and shared effort
- Emotional regulation is a skill, not a fixed trait — built gradually like marathon training
- Relationship situation with ex: reframed as understandable given shared history and loneliness, not a moral failure

## Tools Recommended

- Mindfulness before decisions: breathing, yoga, or a run to reach calm first
- Give decisions time: sit with uncertainty, return the next day rather than panicking
- Journaling: write pros/cons and values; refer back to learn from patterns
- Gratitude practice: 3 things daily, even small ones — trains brain to notice positives
