## Why this matters

20+ years of external messaging (sister calling her dumb, controlling father, frequent moving) created a belief system that wasn't self-chosen. This is about reclaiming that.

## Core affirmation

> "I no longer operate from guilt and shame. I operate from peace and love for myself."

## What's working

- Affirmations were consistent during school — routine helps
- Friends and others already see the potential Elizabeth doesn't always see in herself
- Already ahead of where parents were at her age

## Obstacles

- Home environment creates "noise" that drowns out positive self-talk
- Sister's opinion still carries too much weight in internal dialogue
- Comparison to others with more experience undermines current progress
