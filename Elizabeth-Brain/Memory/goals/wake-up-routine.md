## Why this matters

How the morning goes sets the day. Gym before screens is a non-negotiable personal rule. The 8am goal was set explicitly in therapy (May 12) — current wake time was 11am, target is a 3-hour shift.

## The anchor sequence

1. 8:00 AM — Wake up
2. Breakfast + shower
3. Gym / sports / movement
4. 9:00–10:30 AM — Two-hour deep work lock-in (no "maybe" language, no distractions)

## Obstacles

- Home environment makes it easy to sleep in
- Not in the city (no gym nearby without driving)
- Family interruptions break the schedule

## What's working

- Written schedule > mental schedule (therapy insight: commit to paper, not just thought)
- Treating schedule like classroom time — no interruptions allowed
