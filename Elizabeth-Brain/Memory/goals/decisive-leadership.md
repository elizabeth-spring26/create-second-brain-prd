## Why this matters

Wishy-washy decision-making is the biggest weakness flagged across multiple sessions. Being decisive matters more than being right. Operating as a business owner — not just a student — requires owning the call.

## The pattern to break

- Seek validation from friends before deciding
- Waver easily when someone pushes back
- Invite others to decide by asking for constant input
- Use "maybe" language instead of committing

## What to do instead

- Reach a calm state first (breathe, run, yoga) before deciding
- Sit with uncertainty overnight rather than panicking into a decision
- Commit to written plans — no "maybe" modifications
- Let work and presence speak; stop over-explaining

## Active listening (linked skill)

- Stop repeating yourself to someone in a first impression
- Listen fully before responding
