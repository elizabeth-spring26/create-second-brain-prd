## Pillar Descriptions

### Fitness
Goal: Show up for your body every day. The gym is non-negotiable — it sets the tone for everything else.
- Minimum: any intentional workout (gym, run, walk with intention)
- Ideal: strength session or cardio at the gym before 10 AM

### Deep Work
Goal: Move the needle on something that matters — skills, projects, or output.
- Minimum: one meaningful task completed or one hour of focused work
- Ideal: a block of 2+ uninterrupted hours on a priority project

### Connections
Goal: Relationships compound. One touch per day keeps the network warm.
- Minimum: one reply, check-in, or follow-up to a real person
- Ideal: proactive outreach to someone you haven't talked to in 30+ days

### Evening Ritual
Goal: Close the loop. Reflection turns experience into growth.
- Minimum: 5 minutes of journaling or prayer
- Ideal: 15+ minutes — what happened, what you learned, what tomorrow needs

### Finances
Goal: No financial surprises. Awareness is the first step to control.
- Minimum: glance at account balances via Plaid
- Ideal: review transactions, categorize anything unusual, note one insight
